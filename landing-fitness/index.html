FAQ

Good questions

Do I need a gym?

No. Every programme has a gym version and a home version using dumbbells, bands or just your bodyweight. Tell me what you have on the intake call.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Day One is built for people who have never trained. Every exercise has a video demo, and you can send me clips for feedback.

How much time will I need?

Most sessions take 30–60 minutes. The weekly check-in takes about ten minutes.

What if it isn't working?

You get a full refund in the first week, no questions. After that, I rebuild your plan from your numbers, and you can cancel any time.

Can you work around injuries?

Often, yes. I modify movements and loads around niggles. For anything medical, I'll ask you to check with your doctor first.
