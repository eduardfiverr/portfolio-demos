Good questions

You might be wondering

Will it hurt?

Very rarely. We use numbing gel before every injection, work slowly, and agree a hand signal so you can pause whenever you like. If something is uncomfortable, we stop.

I'm really nervous. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will give you a longer first appointment, a quiet room and as much explanation (or distraction) as you want. We also offer gentle sedation for more complex work.

How much will it cost before I commit?

You get a written plan with every price before any treatment. The price builder above shows typical costs, and your dentist confirms the exact figure after your exam.

Do you take insurance, and can I pay monthly?

We claim directly from most major UK dental insurers, and offer 0% monthly plans on treatment over £300. Lumen Club members also get 10% off.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us before 11am on a weekday and we will see you the same day. Outside those hours, leave a message and we will phone you first thing.

Do you see children?

Of course. Children are welcome from their first tooth. Their first check-ups are short, friendly, and good practice for a lifetime of easy visits.
