Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free. Booking guarantees your time and your barber, especially on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays.

How do I cancel or change a booking?

Reply to the reminder text or tap “Cancel” in our Telegram bot. It is free up to 3 hours before your slot.

Can you cut kids' hair?

Yes, from age four. Under-16s get €5 off any haircut Monday to Wednesday.

Which products do you use?

Professional clippers, straight razors with fresh blades for every shave, and unscented oils for sensitive skin on request.

How do I pay?

In the shop after your cut, by card or cash. We do not take payment online.
